By default, each card has the required attributions on the back. I've bundled the .kra files (import into Krita) so you can customize the colors or change up the designs. 
If you remove the attribution text from the cards, you must add them somewhere else in the game such as a credit screen.

ATTRIBUTION:

The high-res SCP logo on all keycards is from "Dr. Mackenzie's Sketchbook" by Aelanna, from the SCP Wiki. Source: https://scpwiki.com/dr-mackenzie-s-sketchbook

The keycard mesh and textures were created by Destroyanad on Itch.io
